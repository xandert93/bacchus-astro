## Finding your way around

The sidebar is in sections:

- **Sales**: enquiries, bookings, quotes and the waitlist. Most days start
  here.
- **Venue**: packages and menus, spaces, and dates the venue is closed.
- **Website**: the gallery, testimonials, guide articles, FAQs and suppliers.
- **Restaurant**: the restaurant menu, not yet on the website.
- **Settings**: the wedding policy (deposit, VAT, timings) and the site's
  contact details.

Each list is split by stage, in the order work moves through it, with an
"All" list at the bottom.

## Enquiries and bookings

An enquiry and the booking it becomes are the same record. Change its
**Status** as it moves along; nothing needs copying.

| Status              | Means                                                     | On the public calendar |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| New Enquiry         | Arrived from the website. Nobody has replied yet.         | Enquiries received     |
| In Discussion       | We've replied: questions, a viewing, a tasting, a quote.  | Enquiries received     |
| Awaiting Deposit    | The client accepted the quote and has the deposit link.   | Enquiries received     |
| Confirmed           | The deposit has arrived. The date is theirs.              | Booked                 |
| Completed           | The event has happened. Set automatically after the date. | Booked                 |
| Declined by Bacchus | We said no, for example because the date was taken.       | Available              |
| Withdrawn by Client | The client didn't go ahead, before paying anything.       | Available              |
| Cancelled           | It was confirmed, then fell through.                      | Available              |

Good to know:

- **Only weddings affect the public calendar**, since there's one wedding a
  day. Other events can share a date.
- **Several couples can enquire about the same date.** The first to pay their
  deposit gets it; the others are told automatically.
- **Give a reason** whenever you decline or cancel. If the couple asks later,
  that's what we'll look back at.
- **Cancelling a confirmed wedding** frees the date and emails anyone on the
  waitlist for it.

## Quotes

1. **Create** a quote from Quotes. It gets its reference automatically, with
   today's VAT rate and deposit percentage from the wedding policy.
2. **Add the lines.** For anything priced per person or per item, enter the
   quantity and the unit price; the line total, subtotal, total, VAT, price
   per person and deposit all work themselves out. For a flat fee, leave the
   quantity and unit price empty and type the total. For something included
   at no charge (chairs, say), enter a unit price of 0: the client sees
   "Included". Name lines plainly ("Banquet – Dahlia", "Cocktail bar"): the
   quantity already shows what's per person.
   - **Prepared By** is filled in with your name; the quote email is signed
     with it.
   - **Notes for the Client** appear with the quote on their booking page.
     **Internal Notes** never do.
   - **Wedding Cake**: the tiers and flavour of the cake that comes with the
     meal. It shows under the meal line.
   - **Chairs and bar pieces** aren't lines: when you send the quote, it
     adds the range included for its guests (e.g. "32–48 chairs and 1–2 bar
     pieces"), from Settings, Wedding Policy.
3. **Send it**, then set its status to **Sent**. Everything else on the quote
   locks, so it always matches what the client received.
4. **To change a sent quote**, create a new one and set the old one to
   **Superseded**.

Statuses after that: **Accepted** when the client says yes, **Declined by
Client** if they say no, and **Expired** once its "valid until" date passes.

## The waitlist

When a wedding date is taken, couples can ask to be told if it frees up.
They confirm their email address first. If the booking is cancelled, everyone
waiting for that date is emailed automatically.

## Reviews

**A week after every event**, the client is emailed automatically, asking
how it went. Their review arrives in **Website → Testimonials → Awaiting
review**, and you're emailed too. Nothing appears on the website until
someone publishes it.

To publish one:

1. Check they ticked permission to publish ("Their Name Being Used"). If
   they didn't, set it to **Withheld**: it's private feedback.
2. Highlight one phrase with **Gold Emphasis**; the website shows it in
   gold.
3. Set the status to **Published** and publish.

**Google reviews matter most**: they're what couples check, and they help
Bacchus show up in Google's map results. One rule, from Google itself:
**ask every client the same way, never only the happy ones.** Sending only
satisfied clients to Google ("review gating") breaks Google's rules, and
Google can remove the reviews or suspend the listing. The automatic email
already asks everyone. If you ask anyone by hand, ask everyone, and never
suggest an unhappy client keep it off Google.

## Greyed-out fields

A greyed-out field can't be edited. Either it's worked out automatically (a
quote's totals, its reference), or the record is locked (a sent quote). You
can still select and copy what's in it.
